PhoneAppDeck launches on Google Play

Build, test, and ship apps from your phone. PhoneAppDeck brings the development flow around GitHub, AI-assisted coding, review, preview, build, and release into a mobile-first workspace.

Keep development moving away from your desk

PhoneAppDeck is designed for developers who already use GitHub and AI-assisted tools but do not always want to return to a PC just to check a pull request, review a change, inspect a preview, or follow a build.

Rather than trying to squeeze a desktop IDE onto a small screen, the app focuses on the handoffs that happen repeatedly during modern app development: understand the project, ask for a change, review the result, verify the UI, run the build, and continue toward release.

Four areas, one development flow

Home

See connection status, recent project activity, and the next action from one place.

Development

Move between your project and external AI development services for implementation work.

Preview

Review code changes and mobile UI previews before moving on.

Release

Follow GitHub Actions, Android and iOS builds, and the path toward store publication.

Android is live; iOS is under review

The Android version is now available on Google Play. The iOS version has also been submitted to the App Store and is currently under review. We will publish another update when its availability changes.
